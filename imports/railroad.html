Indiana Railroad

Road Operations
View Subcategory Details

The Indianapolis Division is the original INRD trackage (former ICG) between Lis, Illinois, and Indianapolis, Indiana. The Chicago Divison is the former Canadian Pacific Latta Sub trackage between Terre Haute, Indiana, and Bedford, Indiana, which was taken over by the Indiana Railroad in May 2006. The major yard, Hiawatha Yard, is located in Jasonville, Indiana (Greene County).
